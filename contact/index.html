Van Vlaenderen · Collaboration

Connect With the Project

Genealogy is a collaborative journey. Whether you have a question, a correction, or a piece of the puzzle to share, we look forward to hearing from you.

Why Reach Out?

⚜
You carry the name
If your surname is Van Vlaenderen — in any spelling — we want to know your story. Every branch of the family matters.
⚜
You have documents or photographs
Old letters, birth certificates, photographs, land records — any document connected to the Van Vlaenderen family is valuable.
⚜
You have taken a DNA test
If you have tested with AncestryDNA, 23andMe, or FamilyTreeDNA and have Van Vlaenderen ancestry, your results could connect the branches.
⚜
You have local knowledge
If you live in or near the Meetjesland and know stories about the Van Vlaenderen family or the mill at Vinderhoute, please share them.
⚜
You have a question
Genealogical research can be confusing. If you have a question about the Van Vlaenderen family or how to research your own roots, ask away.
⚜Your personal information is never shared, sold, or published without your explicit consent. This is a private family research project.

Send a Message
